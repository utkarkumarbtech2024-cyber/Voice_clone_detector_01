Call Shield: Real-Time AI Voice Clone Defense

Slide 1 of 10
  • Product Brief: Continuous acoustic deepfake and voice-clone interception during live phone calls.
  • Key Value Proposition: Zero-latency streaming analysis with actionable alerts before social engineering damage occurs.
  • Architecture Philosophy: 100% In-Memory Processing with strict Zero Audio Retention.
🎙️ Speaker Notes
"Good morning/afternoon everyone. Today we are presenting Call Shield—a real-time acoustic defense system engineered to protect individuals, executives, and organizations from the fastest-growing form of social engineering: AI voice clones and deepfake phone scams."

The Threat: Rise of AI Voice Cloning Scams

Slide 2 of 10
  • 3-Second Cloning: State-of-the-art generative voice models clone any voice using just seconds of social media audio.
  • Urgency & Impersonation: Targeted CEO wire fraud, bank verification impersonation, and family emergency ransom scams.
  • Why Firewalls Fail: Voice cloning attacks human psychology and hearing directly, bypassing traditional perimeter defenses.
  • Financial Toll: Over $1.1 Billion lost globally in imposter calls in 2024–2025; 83% of victims cannot distinguish clones by ear.
🎙️ Speaker Notes
"With modern generative AI, a scammer only needs a short Instagram clip to clone a voice. Once they dial in, the victim has zero technical safety net. Traditional security models inspect network packets, but none inspect the acoustic signature of the voice in real time."

Solution: Call Shield Architecture

Slide 3 of 10
  • Active Remote Listening: Intercepts the remote caller's audio stream without touching local or private audio.
  • Continuous Acoustic Scoring: Evaluates vocal periodicity, spectral flatness, and vocoder artifacts every second.
  • Progressive Warning System: Transitions smoothly from OK (Authentic) → WARNING → CRITICAL ALERT.
  • Omnichannel Ingestion: Built-in support for Twilio telephony streams, browser microphone Worklets, and audio file uploads.
🎙️ Speaker Notes
"Call Shield acts as an intelligent acoustic firewall between the caller and the receiver. It analyzes the voice stream chunk-by-chunk and alerts the user the moment synthetic vocal patterns are detected."

Signal Processing Pipeline

Slide 4 of 10
[Inbound Stream] ──► [ITU-T G.711 Decode] ──► [Resampler 8k->16k] ──► [RMS Normalize -26 dBFS] │ [Non-blocking Alerts] ◄── [AlertDecider (k/n)] ◄── [Inference Engine] ◄── [Sliding 4s Window]
  • Telephony Transcoder: Hardware-verified ITU-T G.711 µ-law decode and encode tables.
  • FFT-Preserving Resampler: Converts 8 kHz telephone audio to 16 kHz model rate without spectral leakage.
  • Loudness Normalization: Scaled to -26 dBFS target RMS for gain-invariant neural classification.
  • Sliding Windows: 4.0-second sliding windows with 1.0-second strides; first score ready in just 2.0s.
🎙️ Speaker Notes
"Under the hood, audio is ingested through dual channels: browser microphones or telephony PBX feeds. We resample, normalize, and slice the audio into sliding 4-second windows that are scored continuously without blocking the event loop."

Decision Engine & Latching Protection

Slide 5 of 10
  • k-out-of-n Rolling Decider: Analyzes the trailing 6 seconds (n=6). 4 or more flagged windows trigger an ALERT.
  • Latching Safety Mechanism: Once ALERT is reached, the state latches for the call session so scammers cannot reset warnings with pauses.
  • Human-Centric Defensive Phrasing: "This voice may be AI-generated. Hang up and call back on a number you know."
  • Haptic Alerts: Physical vibration pulses trigger on mobile devices during critical threats.
🎙️ Speaker Notes
"One-off classification is prone to false alarms. Call Shield implements a rolling 4-out-of-6 latching decider. Once an alert is triggered, it locks in for the duration of the call so the user is never tricked into false security by a brief pause."

Enterprise Security & Zero Retention (Phase 7)

Slide 7 of 10
  • Zero Disk Persistence: Automated test snapshots confirm zero audio files or state are ever written to disk or temp storage.
  • Log Sanitization: Strict automated filters guarantee raw waveforms, base64 chunks, and tokens never leak into logs.
  • DoS & Flood Protections: Hard caps on frame size (64 KB) and session duration with policy violation disconnects (code 1008).
  • Graceful Shutdown: Lifespan manager cleanly terminates active WebSockets and cancels in-flight inference tasks.
🎙️ Speaker Notes
"In cybersecurity, privacy is non-negotiable. Many detection tools record audio to third-party clouds, creating massive wiretapping and GDPR liabilities. Call Shield processes audio entirely in volatile memory and discards it immediately after scoring."
